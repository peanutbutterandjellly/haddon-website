THE WESTERN RANGE is about being a cowboy! The A side is more energetic, while the B side is a bit more relaxed and intricate. I made it over the span of a year and a half or so, and you may be able to hear my style change throughout the record.

Alt Text: "THE WESTERN RANGE album cover. There's a border/frame made of the single cover, which is like a bunch of little squares. Red and yellow and blue and green. There are some very colorful cowboy boots. I have never been able to find a pair of cowboy boots that fit my ginormous feet. There's a piano accordion keyboard with colored accidentals. A guitar. There is a cowgirl and a cowboy dancing. The cowgirl has angel wings and her hair is full of the moon and stars. They're holding each other in giant hands. Art by Ginger Bolton."

THE WESTERN RANGE
